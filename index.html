Everything you need to know about getting started with Meta AI.

Meta AI is your built-in, intelligent assistant powered by state-of-the-art Llama language and vision foundation models. It helps you explore topics, generate ideas, create high-quality photorealistic images, summarize texts, and get real-time info directly across Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, Ray-Ban Meta glasses, and on the web at meta.ai.
Yes, Meta AI is free to access and use across all supported Meta platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger) and via your browser on meta.ai. There is no subscription required to interact with the assistant or create images.
You can access Meta AI simply by tapping the iridescent ring icon inside WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Messenger, or Facebook search bars. You can also visit meta.ai on any browser, or simply say "Hey Meta" if you are wearing Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses.
Meta AI seamlessly lives directly where you communicate every day. You don't have to switch apps or copy-paste between windows. You can tag @MetaAI into a group chat on WhatsApp to vote on dinner spots, animate images on Instagram, and even ask questions about what you are seeing in front of you through Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses.
Simply start your prompt with the word "Imagine" (e.g., "Imagine a futuristic electric motorcycle on Mars in sunset light") or tap the image generator tool. Meta AI will synthesize multiple variations in seconds. You can then request adjustments like "Make it black and white" or "Add a neon halo".
